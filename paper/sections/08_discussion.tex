\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Two mechanisms, two different jobs.}
The results separate an effect we set out to measure from one we did not. Exposure to diverse observation processes
is what reduces sensitivity to a change of observation process, and it does so without an in-distribution cost.
Privileged observation self-distillation instead improves absolute accuracy, on clean and shifted conditions
alike, in the settings where the task is information limited, and leaves relative degradation unchanged. Conflating
the two would be easy, because both mechanisms involve sparse views of the same history, and it would make the
paper's central claim untestable. We therefore state them separately, and we do not claim that the complete
configuration is uniformly the best choice: on PhysioNet the best shifted error comes from augmentation with a
harsher mining pool, and on USHCN at one month the best clean and mean shifted error come from augmentation plus
distillation without mining.

\paragraph{Reading the degradation ratio.}
Eq.~\ref{eq:degradation} normalises by a model's own clean score, which makes a uniformly weaker model look robust.
USHCN-1mo is the clear case: the baseline's ratio, $+1.9\%$, is close to the complete configuration's $+1.5\%$,
yet its error is higher at every condition, and its clean error ($0.53787$) exceeds the complete configuration's
error \emph{under shift} ($0.52871$). A second failure mode concerns the choice of metric. On P12, cross-entropy
improves under mild shifts while AUROC and AUPRC fall, so a degradation ratio computed on cross-entropy is
negative and uninterpretable there. We compute classification degradation on $1-\mathrm{AUROC}$ throughout, which
makes P12 an ordinary mild case ($+11.4\%$ baseline, $+7.2\%$ with the method) rather than an anomaly, but the
episode is a reminder that the summary metric has to be chosen before it is trusted.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
Every number here is single-seed and preliminary. The protocol calls for five paired seeds, and the smaller
differences in Tables~\ref{tab:components}, \ref{tab:posd} and \ref{tab:unseen} are within the range we would
expect seeds to move; the mining comparison in particular rests on a $0.00006$\,MSE margin and should not be read
as established. Our observation processes are synthetic re-observations of real records, which is what makes the
targets well defined but is weaker evidence than a shift between two genuinely different acquisition environments.
Compositions are limited to pairs, and the unseen families are drawn from the same nine-primitive vocabulary as the
training pool. The two backbones are tied to tasks rather than crossed with them, so architecture and task are not
separated. USHCN is not min-max normalised by its published pipeline, so its errors are in raw units and not
comparable in magnitude to the other forecasting settings. Finally, the teacher in our experiments observes the
untouched record; we do not vary the richness of the teacher's view, so we cannot say how much of the distillation
gain depends on the size of the teacher--student observation gap.
